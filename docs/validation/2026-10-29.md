## Differences

### 1. Period prices: gold vs EMI `W2QJHA` (explained)

On 44–45 of 48 periods, gold period price equals EMI weighted price within $0.01/MWh.

The outliers share one cause. EMI `W2QJHA` is built from RTD cases with an **effective start** and a **duration**. Dispatch Energy Prices (gold's input) omit a short case at the period start. Rule B then applies the first dispatch publish from the period start ([ADR-0012](../adr/0012-gold-prices.md)). That first publish is a different price from the omitted RTD case.

Worked example, Ōtāhuhu, period 7 (03:00–03:30 NZDT):

| Clock | EMI `W2QJHA` | Dispatch Energy Prices (silver) | Gold rule B |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 03:00:00–03:03:40 (220 s) | $3.06 (case `281302026101330899`) | no row | first publish $22.64, applied from 03:00:00 |
| 03:03:40 onward | $22.64, $22.63, $3.18, … | same prices | same prices |

Weight of the gap: \(220 / 1800 \times (22.64 - 3.06) = 2.39\) $/MWh. Measured gold − EMI = $2.40. Periods 13, 15 (Ōtāhuhu only, 26 s at $26.05 vs first publish $26.88) and 28 are the same pattern with shorter leading cases.

This is not a gold bug. It is the owner choice to use Dispatch Energy Prices plus rule B ([#9](https://github.com/joshmcarthur/gridwatch/issues/9), [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-gold-prices.md)). Matching `W2QJHA` exactly would need the RTD case list, not the dispatch publish file.

The ~$0.05/MWh gap in the daily mean is these four periods averaged over 48 (period 7 dominates).

### 2. National generation: gold vs EMI `W_GG_C` (explained)

Gold primary generation is 112.357 GWh. `W_GG_C` New Zealand is 121.742 GWh. Difference 9.385 GWh (7.7% of the EMI figure).

These are different products:

- Gold / Generation_MD: metered grid injections mapped to known plant ([Generation_MD notes](https://www.emi.ea.govt.nz/Wholesale/Datasets/Generation/Generation_MD)).
- `W_GG_C`: reconciled generation injected into the grid, complete through 31 October 2026 per the report notes.

Evidence from the `W_GG_C` node CSV (208 nodes in the default series, 118.651 GWh) against Generation_MD by `POC_Code`:

- 125 EMI nodes are absent from Generation_MD and hold 4.093 GWh (embedded and distribution-connected generation at GXPs).
- Three Generation_MD POCs are absent or zero in that node series, including `KIW2201` Kaiwera Downs Stage 2 at 2.726 GWh of wind (`WIN`). The node report shows 0.000 GWh there.
- At POCs present in both, reconciled energy is often higher than the plant-mapped meters (Huntly `HLY2201` is 0.696 GWh in `W_GG_C` and 0 in Generation_MD on this day).

Gold follows Generation_MD. The national gap is a source-definition gap, not a publisher error. EMI has no fuel-mix report to compare the fuel split against a second methodology.

## SQL

Set `DATA_ROOT` to the Gridwatch data directory (the same tree the publisher writes). Example: `DATA_ROOT=/path/to/gridwatch/data`.

Gold queries do not reimplement publisher logic. They read the gold tables from [#16](https://github.com/joshmcarthur/gridwatch/issues/16) and [#17](https://github.com/joshmcarthur/gridwatch/issues/17).

### Generation by fuel (gold)

```sql
SELECT DISTINCT analysis_version, inputs_complete
FROM read_parquet('${DATA_ROOT}/gold/generation_by_fuel_day/trading_date=2026-10-29/data.parquet');

SELECT fuel_category, island, megawatt_hours, average_megawatts, inputs_complete
FROM read_parquet('${DATA_ROOT}/gold/generation_by_fuel_day/trading_date=2026-10-29/data.parquet')
ORDER BY fuel_category, island NULLS LAST;

SELECT fuel_category,
       round(sum(megawatt_hours) / 1000.0, 6) AS gwh
FROM read_parquet('${DATA_ROOT}/gold/generation_by_fuel_day/trading_date=2026-10-29/data.parquet')
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY 1;

SELECT round(sum(CASE WHEN fuel_category <> 'battery' THEN megawatt_hours ELSE 0 END) / 1000.0, 6) AS primary_gwh,
       round(sum(megawatt_hours) / 1000.0, 6) AS all_gwh,
       bool_and(inputs_complete) AS complete
FROM read_parquet('${DATA_ROOT}/gold/generation_by_fuel_day/trading_date=2026-10-29/data.parquet');
```
